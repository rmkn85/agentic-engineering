# Steer delivery

The existing coordinator acts as the Technical Delivery Lead; each autonomous epic owner steers its own domain. Continue toward the authorized outcome, not automatically along the same approach. This is a responsibility, not a mandatory additional agent or approval layer. An explicitly assigned persistent steering reviewer retains goals and prior corrections, reviews at useful checkpoints or a requested schedule, and leaves ordinary next-step decisions with the owners. See the [role and lifetime distinctions](../../docs/local-codex/subagents.md#worker-lifetime-and-integration).

Load this procedure for substantial iterative work. Review before selecting work or replanning, before the next substantial batch and at meaningful iteration/commit, handoff, pause, resume, proposed completion, material scope/evidence change, recurring failure or allocation boundary. Do not wait for a commit while an uncommitted loop consumes resources. Coalesce adjacent events with unchanged evidence; do not trigger a new review solely because its own record was saved.

A scope or checkpoint request stops new implementation while the coordinator reconciles current work and updates affected worker briefs; preserve in-flight work and resume only within the clarified scope. Routine failure recovery belongs to the tool or owning worker; escalate only an unresolved decision. Stop unchanged structural or permission retries until a specific relevant correction is available.

## Check, decide, act

1. **Recover purpose before selecting work.** From the current requirements, task, owned changes, previous checkpoint and decisive evidence, recover the controlling purpose, intended user outcome, proven capabilities and remaining outcome gap. Retrieve agreed architecture and the owning implementation before proposing a local workaround. Preserve valid qualification; distinguish provider proof, consumer adoption and full outcome acceptance. Distinguish requirements from assumptions and implementation choices, including genuinely new requirements. Reassess a retained plan after resume without restarting valid discovery.
2. **Compare reality, not activity.** Identify verified capability, removed obstructions, decision-changing findings, regressions and new obligations. Compare both the latest increment and the principal user-outcome gap. Check whether the previous steering action was applied and reduced the remaining gap; if not or unknown, steer the next action accordingly. Use the [outcome and complete-operation scorecard](../../docs/measurement/metrics.md#outcome-and-complete-operation-scorecard); activity and diagnostic success do not receive product-progress credit.
3. **Review resources and authority.** Recheck the next phase's executor against the [routing policy](../../docs/local-codex/subagents.md#named-model-and-effort-policy), including reused workers and reviewers. At meaningful checkpoints use the [Codex usage API](../../docs/local-codex/subagents.md#usage-reasonableness-checkpoints) to compare depletion with rough expected work/pace and reset horizon, without a universal percentage or polling cadence. Investigate surprises before attributing them to our execution or the platform; shared-account samples alone cannot decide. Slow/stop when warranted and obtain owner approval before materially accelerating spending beyond existing explicit authorization. Select the next executor from the canonical routing policy; a prior model choice does not bind the next phase. Ultra requires the current explicit exhaust-before-reset instruction. For allowance-bound work, observed exhaustion, reset or the owner deadline ends authorization: stop/cancel affected work, preserve state and report unconfirmed cancellation. Paid credits do not extend it. Keep time, human effort, tokens by model, tool calls, expenditure and rework separate, including coordination and parallel workers; unknown measurements stay unknown.
4. **Select from the gap, then challenge the action.** Explain how the next bounded work advances the controlling outcome or resolves a decision that blocks it before choosing among ready tasks. Readiness permits consideration; convenience, queue position and sunk cost do not establish priority. Do not select a convenient task and justify it afterward. Preserve dependency semantics; when the parent outcome is blocked, continue justified independent authorized work and retain the blocked acceptance gate. Would the choice still be justified from current evidence without sunk-cost pressure? Reconsider executor, model/effort, useful count and lifetime, including zero new agents; a saved app selection is not a routing decision. Check ownership/worktree collisions, pending PR integration, stale caches and recurring permission requests. Retire completed workers, preserve evidence before clearing scratch, and replace repeated escalation with a reviewed native activity binding. Consider simplifying or a discriminating experiment. Preserve useful work. An overrun is not automatic failure; learning can justify persistence when it changes a decision.
5. **Decide and execute.** Choose CONTINUE, STEER, BLOCKED or COMPLETE. Before substantial work, record the outcome, obstacle, expected effect, baseline and conditions, and whether to adopt, investigate or abandon the intervention in the existing task. For actionable unfinished work, select and execute the next coherent action with expected evidence and a reconsideration condition. When a genuine remaining budget is finite or prerequisites keep expanding, choose the smallest action that advances the outcome or resolves the blocking decision. An epic owner continues autonomously after checkpoints and milestones through actual accepted delivery; no routine renewal is needed. Estimates guide replanning, not termination: do not stop on an invented token/time budget or call estimated use exhausted account allowance. Preserve original estimates, cumulative observations and actual user/runtime/resource limits across task splits, new agents and resumes. A finite diagnostic experiment may end while the owner changes route or continues independent work. A forecast is neither spending authorization nor a hard cap.
6. **Close the feedback loop.** Record the decision as specified below, replace the superseded next action in the existing task/handoff, and send material corrections to affected active workers through supported controls. Coordinate shared-contract changes through one owner. Verify adoption and effect at the next checkpoint. Do not stop at advice or request a routine user nudge.

## Research and questions follow the decision

Before ranking research topics or tools, identify the actual design decision, what is already established, and which missing evidence could change the choice. Retrieve relevant sources, synthesize their implications and conflicts, then choose the smallest useful investigation or tool. File retrieval is not research synthesis; fitting text is not selecting the content the user needs; a new presentation tool does not supply autonomous judgment. An authorized bounded experiment is useful when its result changes the decision, including a negative result.

Resolve questions from existing requirements, architecture, implementation, evidence or authorized experiments first. Ask the owner only for a genuinely missing preference, consequential choice outside delegated discretion or required authorization. State remaining uncertainty; do not invent an answer or turn an ordinary engineering choice into compulsory review.

## Authority and proportion

Choose implementation, sequencing, necessary supporting work, verification and executor changes within the existing contract and model/resource policy. Do not remove requirements, lower acceptance, add optional product scope, widen permissions or exceed owner limits. Escalate only the specific unresolved authority/value decision; continue independent authorized work. COMPLETE requires evidence for the requested scope and stops optional polishing.

Use one independent reviewer for consequential ambiguity, conflicting evidence or repeated failure when fresh judgment can change the decision. Prefer the existing persistent steering reviewer when its independence and retained context fit; its review is a focused intervention, not termination of its ongoing role. Otherwise use a bounded specialist or keep ordinary review local. Route by the actual reasoning and modality through the canonical policy; review is not automatically a demanding specialist assignment. "Reviewer" does not imply High or Ultra; stronger effort needs the named policy's scoped authorization. Give the reviewer authoritative requirements and the artifact without the builder's preferred verdict; require concrete counterexamples or decisive evidence. A solo agent's self-review is not independent. Evidence resolves disagreement, not vote count. Do not rerun unchanged checks or create a standing panel.

## Decision record

Keep programme orientation recoverable through the existing authoritative task/handoff and its linked requirements, architecture and evidence. Where adopted, use the [project work routes](../../docs/adoption/project-work.md) to recover the objective and task detail; read canonical history only when needed. Update a stale next action there, not in a parallel tracker. No new fields, status store or per-task form is required; populated fields alone do not establish sound judgment.

Keep material decisions and their effects in the existing task history or a coherent commit-message body, coalesced with meaningful work. A routine continuation needs only enough evidence and next action to resume; it does not need a separate record or commit. For a material change, retain the trigger, rationale, tradeoff, uncertainty, owner and expected check; record the observed effect when known. Do not rewrite the original rationale with hindsight.

Keep decision records out of source code, code comments, configurations and generated product artifacts. Do not create a parallel ledger or log-only commit loop. Link detailed tool evidence; do not copy private transcripts, credentials or raw dumps. If the normal record cannot be written, preserve it in an authorized task/handoff or commit route and report the missing persistence without claiming it succeeded.

[Record examples and adoption](../../docs/execution/delivery-steering.md) and [behavioral trials](../../experiments/delivery-steering.md) are optional detail. The procedure above is self-contained. This policy is an engineering hypothesis to evaluate, not proof of automatic invocation or measured savings.
