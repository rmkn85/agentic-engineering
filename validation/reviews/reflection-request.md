# Original request and execution boundary

Implement executable reflection and recurrence prevention in the methodology
repository. Agents must preserve acceptance, reject stale success, test
corrective decisions, and challenge whether their selected work satisfies the
original user request.

Preserve the complete acceptance contract across sessions, with explicit
authority for revisions. Bind success to the contract, validator and relevant
inputs. Audit freshness without rerunning checks; reject concurrent mutations
and treat legacy receipts as unverified until refreshed.

Use existing goal and findings records. Discrepancies must trigger executable
correction with a hypothesis, prediction, discriminating maintained check and
before/after evidence. Preserve failed attempts and unresolved obligations.
Reopen recurring or invalidated findings, including earlier declined findings.
Healthy tasks should not acquire mandatory reflection paperwork.

Derive acceptance independently from original intent and failure evidence before
reviewing the proposed contract or code. Require that review for methodology and
control changes. Connect repository-local startup/resume and Stop adapters to
the same CLI controls, preserving host restrictions. Configuration is not proof
of native hook trust or invocation. Keep ordinary instruction reading bounded.

Freeze baseline, candidate, prompts, fixtures and scoring before four matched
baseline/candidate pairs: workflow false completion, a regressed successful
handoff, an independently prepared held-out variation, and a healthy small
change. Use sequential fresh native Codex sessions with gpt-6.1-sol/Medium,
native instruction discovery and unchanged surrounding configuration. Keep
evaluators outside worker repositories and retain every failed, interrupted,
assisted or inconclusive attempt. Score consumer outcomes and truthful claims.

Verify automated regressions, mutation proofs, native integration, guidance
budget, findings, cold/stale contributor routes, independent correctness and
privacy. Use one coherent branch and PR, merge after checks and reasoned review,
verify merged CI and synchronize the canonical checkout. Preserve unrelated work
and retire only owned preserved branches/worktrees.

Neutral repositories are the experimental scope; live project deliveries are
excluded. Comparative superiority is not a merge condition: both arms succeeding
is inconclusive. Native execution being unavailable is a concrete blocker, not
permission to substitute coached prompts and call them normal-workflow proof.
The result is bounded evidence and tested known protections, never a guarantee
that future issues cannot be missed.
